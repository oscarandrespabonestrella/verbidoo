# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in the Verbidoo community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Verbidoo is made for children. We hold ourselves to the standard of behaviour we would want them to see.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind, patient and welcoming, especially to newcomers and to people writing in a language that isn't their first
- Respecting regional language varieties: Québec French is as correct as France French, Flemish as Netherlands Dutch
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the children who use the app

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Sharing any personal information about children, including screenshots that show real names or faces
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainer privately through [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/oscarandrespabonestrella/verbidoo/security/advisories/new) or by contacting [@oscarandrespabonestrella](https://github.com/oscarandrespabonestrella) on GitHub. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.
